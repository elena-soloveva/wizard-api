# The Spellbook (API Reference) 📖

This is a complete list of all built-in spells available in WizardAPI.

## Available spells

- **fireball(target: str)** — Launch a fireball at your enemy.
- **heal(target: str)** — Restore HP to a friendly target.
- **summon_familiar(type: str)** — Summon a magical pet (e.g., owl, cat).
- **invisibility(duration: int)** — Make yourself unseen.
- **teleport(destination: str)** — Instantly move to another location.
- **shield(strength: int)** — Protect yourself from attacks.

## Example:

```python
caster.cast("teleport", destination="Arcane Tower")
```
